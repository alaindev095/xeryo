The challenge

Students need accessible ways to discover internship opportunities relevant to their training.

Technical and vocational training prepares students for practical work, and an internship is often where that preparation meets a real workplace. Finding the right opportunity should not depend on chance.

The approach

The platform aims to make it easier to find opportunities and connect with participating companies.

Umunara Internship brings students and companies into one place, so that opportunities are easier to discover and easier to reach. It is initially focused on Rwanda.

Who it serves

Built for everyone in the internship journey.

Students

TVET students seeking internship opportunities and a way to connect with participating companies.

Companies

Companies offering internship positions that want to be easier for TVET students to find.

Schools

Schools supporting their students during internship periods.

Why it matters

Education and experience, closer together.

Connecting education with practical experience can help students navigate the transition toward professional work.

Umunara Internship does not promise placements, employment or the success of any application. Its purpose is narrower and more honest: to make opportunities easier to discover and to make connections easier to start.

Questions

Good to know.

What is Umunara Internship?

A digital platform designed to help TVET students discover internship opportunities and connect with participating companies. It is the first product built and operated by Xeryo.

Is this website the Umunara Internship application?

No. This is Xeryo’s information page about the product. The application is a separate product; open the Umunara Internship application to use it.

Does Umunara Internship guarantee an internship?

No. The platform is designed to make opportunities easier to find and connections easier to start. It does not guarantee placement, employment or the outcome of any application.

Who is Umunara Internship for?

TVET students looking for internships, companies offering internship positions, and schools that support students during internship periods.

How can my school or company get in touch?

Use the Xeryo contact page to reach the team about school, company or partnership enquiries.
